In this lesson, we are going to understand the journey from revenue to operating income. First thing to remember: revenue is not profit.

```js/0
Revenue - Cost Of Goods Sold (COGS) = Gross Profit
```

COGS, or Cost of Revenue, is the cost that is directly related to revenue generation. If you take cost to produce a product but the product is not sold in the reporting period, the cost is not considered part of COGS in that period.

```js/0
Gross Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue
```

Gross Margin tells you how profitable a buiness is.
