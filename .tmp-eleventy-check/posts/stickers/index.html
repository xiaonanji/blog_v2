Multi-Cursor on beginning of line:

1. Place cursor at the beginning of the first line. 2. Hold Alt + Shift and click at the beginning of the last line. 3. Or hold Alt + Shift and press Down Arrow repeatedly

Multi-Cursor on beginning of line:

1. Select all the lines you want to edit. 2. Press Alt + Shift + I — this places a cursor at the end of each selected line. 3. Press Home to move all cursors to the beginning
